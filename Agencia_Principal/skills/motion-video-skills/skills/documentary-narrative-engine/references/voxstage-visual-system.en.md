# VOXSTAGE Visual System

Use this system for all visual beats, finished frames, and animation unless the user explicitly selects another style.

## Visual foundation

**THE STAGE** is an empty, evenly lit 16:9 field of desaturated archival maps and aged-paper texture. Preserve matte fibers, print grain, black-and-white halftone dots, torn edges, and soft paper-layer shadows. Keep the tone serious, calm, and newsroom-authoritative. Forbid glossy CG, plastic 3D, lens flares, gradients, unrelated logos, watermarks, and random glyphs.

## Palette and components

Use only Archival Tan `#C9BB9C`, Ink Black `#1A1A1A`, Halftone Gray `#8C8C8C`, Hot Red `#D62E1F`, and Mustard `#D9A441`. Use red only for registration offsets, underlines, arrows, data cards, or indicators; use mustard only as a sparse accent.

Build figures as black-and-white halftone photo cut-outs with a rough white border and a subtly offset red registration outline. Use bold red counter cards for data, thin-white-border paper cards for archival photographs, and torn paper, map pins, hand-drawn red underlines, and sparse mustard tags as supporting components. Preserve the editorial grid, uniform margins, and generous negative space.

## Motion

Make figure cut-outs enter with a small overshoot and settle, data counters tick upward step by step, and red underlines draw left to right. Limit camera motion to an approximately 2% slow drift. Restrict movement to paper slides, tape presses, stamp hits, tears, and registration offsets; avoid fluid and rubbery deformation.
